💬 Introduction

Welcome to SmartCare! We value your trust and are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information while using our Hospital Management System.

📋 Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Your name, contact details, and medical history.
  • Account Information: Your username, encrypted password, and appointment records.
  • Device Details: IP address, browser type, and operating system to ensure smooth and secure access.

🧠 How We Use Your Information

  • Provide you with better healthcare services 💖
  • Manage your appointments and send updates
  • Keep your account secure from unauthorized access
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations

🔐 Data Security

Your privacy is our priority. We use encryption, firewalls, and other security tools to protect your data. While no system is completely foolproof, we advise you to keep your login details safe and private.

🔄 Sharing of Information

We never sell your personal data. We may share it only:

  • When required by law 🧾
  • With authorized doctors or staff for your care
  • For technical maintenance and system improvements

✅ Your Rights

  • View, update, or delete your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing messages
  • Request a copy of the information we store

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to personalize your experience and monitor system performance. You can adjust your cookie settings anytime through your browser.
